I am a Mechanical Engineering professional with a Master of Engineering degree from the University of Guelph and nearly a decade of experience supporting manufacturing, mechanical design, quality systems, and production operations. My background combines hands-on industrial experience with strong technical expertise in CAD modeling, GD&T, tolerance analysis, manufacturing processes, and engineering documentation. As a Certified SOLIDWORKS Associate (CSWA), I enjoy transforming engineering concepts into practical, manufacturable designs that improve performance, reliability, and production efficiency.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to mechanical design projects, tooling and fixture development, quality improvement initiatives, and manufacturing support in both automotive and industrial environments. My experience spans mechanical design, CNC manufacturing, quality assurance, and control systems education, allowing me to approach engineering challenges from multiple perspectives. I am passionate about continuous learning, problem-solving, and developing innovative mechanical solutions that bridge design, manufacturing, and real-world implementation.
