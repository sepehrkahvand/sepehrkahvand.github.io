# Power Supply Frame and Kitchen Sink

## Power Supply Frame

- **Tools:** SolidWorks Sheet Metal, assemblies, flat-pattern generation, drafting

- **Description:** Developed a sheet-metal power supply frame featuring bend operations, mounting features, and manufacturing-ready flat patterns. The project demonstrates practical enclosure design, assembly integration, and fabrication-oriented engineering practices.

## Kitchen Sink

- **Tools:** SolidWorks Sheet Metal, surface modeling, assemblies

- **Description:** Designed a sheet-metal kitchen sink incorporating realistic forming operations, drainage features, and production-oriented geometry. The project emphasizes manufacturability, sheet-metal design techniques, and practical product development considerations.
