# Tubular Chair

- **Tools:** SolidWorks weldments, structural members, assemblies, drafting

- **Description:** Designed a tubular steel chair using weldment features and structural member profiles. The project demonstrates frame design, weldment cut lists, fabrication-ready documentation, and efficient use of standardized tubing sections. Emphasis was placed on manufacturability, structural integrity, and realistic fabrication practices.
