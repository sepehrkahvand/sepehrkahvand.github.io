# Differential Gearbox

- **Tools:** SolidWorks part modeling, gear modeling, assemblies, mates, motion study, drafting

- **Description:** Developed a compact differential gearbox combining a worm-gear speed reducer with bevel-type spider gears to split torque between two output shafts. The project demonstrates accurate gear meshing, bearing-supported shafts, realistic power transmission, and assembly integration. Particular attention was given to manufacturability, housing design, shaft alignment, and smooth mechanical operation.
