# Gate Valve

- **Tools:** SolidWorks part modeling, assemblies, drafting

- **Description:** Developed a detailed gate valve assembly consisting of body, bonnet, stem, gate, handwheel, and fastening components. The project demonstrates complex assembly modeling, mechanical fit verification, and industrial equipment design practices. Particular attention was given to component interaction, manufacturability, and realistic mechanical representation.
