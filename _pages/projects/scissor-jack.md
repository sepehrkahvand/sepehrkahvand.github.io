# Scissor Jack

- **Tools:** SolidWorks assemblies, mates, motion study, drafting

- **Description:** Developed a functional scissor jack assembly demonstrating mechanical linkage systems, screw-driven lifting mechanisms, and realistic assembly constraints. Motion studies were used to validate lifting performance and component interaction. The project emphasizes mechanical design principles, manufacturability, and assembly functionality.
